Proconnect Apps & Reports

  • Login to Proconnect, then open the "Developer tools" on the browser and click on the Network tab
  • Navigate to the "Tax Returns" dashboard
  • After the page loads, look for a line starting with "returnstatus" near the bottom of the Network log
  • Right-click the "returnstatus" line
  • Click Copy → Copy as cURL in the pop-up menu that appears
  • Click on "Run Proconnect Report"
